With Spotify connected, Mycelian shows the song you are playing on stream: track name, artist, album art and a progress bar.

What the connection gives you

  • Now playing overlay: the spotify template shows the current song, artist, album art, elapsed and total time, and a progress bar.
  • Live updates: Mycelian asks Spotify for playback about every 5 seconds and pushes changes to the overlay.
  • Pause handling: album art can turn grayscale when paused, and the overlay can hide when nothing is playing.
  • Spore Studio: the Now playing (Spotify) event and the now playing widget use the same data. See Data Feed.

Spotify has no chatbot variables.

Add the overlay to OBS

  1. Open SettingsAbout and find Spotify under Available Source URLs.
  2. Click the copy button, or the OBS button to add it straight to a scene (needs the OBS WebSocket).
  3. If you add it by hand, create a Browser Source in OBS and paste the URL. See OBS Browser Sources.

Music must be playing for the overlay to show anything. Spotify reports playback from any device signed in to your account: desktop app, web player or phone.

If the overlay looks wrong

The overlay is empty

Check that the Spotify tab shows Connected and a song under Current track. If music is paused, Hide When Nothing Playing hides the overlay after the hide delay. Then check the browser source URL and refresh the source in OBS.

Album art is missing

Some tracks, such as local files, have no artwork. The overlay then shows the Default Album Art Image.

Song changes show up late

Mycelian checks Spotify about every 5 seconds, so a new song can take a few seconds to appear. This is expected.

Privacy

  • Mycelian only asks for permission to read playback state and the currently playing track. It cannot control playback or change your library.
  • No listening history is stored.
  • The Client ID, Client Secret and tokens are stored encrypted on this computer.
  • You can remove access any time from your Spotify account under Manage apps.
