The footer shows every connection change as it happens. Pop-ups are only shown when a service settles into a problem or recovers from one, so routine connecting and reconnecting appear here and nowhere else. A service that was just started (opening OBS while Mycelian is running, for example) gets about 30 seconds to connect before a pop-up says it can't. See Notifications.

What the footer looks like

You pick how the badges look in SettingsApp SettingsStatus bar. There are four full looks and four minimal looks. Every look uses the same colors and the same click and hover behavior.

Full badges

Badge styleWhat it shows
Name over status (default)The icon in a small tile with a colored dot on its corner. The name sits above the status word. A badge with a problem gets a tinted background, and one that is not connected is faded.
Soft pillsEach service in a rounded pill with its name and a dot. Working services show no status word; problems tint the pill and say what is wrong.
One segmented barAll services in one bar, split into sections. Like Soft pills, only problems show a status word.
ClassicThe original look: icon, name, dot and an uppercase status pill on every badge.

Minimal badges

Turn on Minimal badges to hide the names and save space. Hover an icon to read its name and status.

Minimal badge styleWhat it shows
Tiles with a status dot (default)Each icon in a small tile with a colored dot on its corner. Tiles with a problem are tinted; services that are not connected are grayed out.
Problems spelled outAll icons in one capsule. Services with a problem, or still connecting, move to the front and show their status word.
Summary and status barsA summary on the left (All connected, Connecting or N need attention) and a thin colored bar under each icon.
Colored ringsThe original minimal look: each icon with a ring in its status color.

Progress badges

Two extra badges appear only while work is running, and disappear when it ends. They keep the Classic look with a text label whatever style you pick, and they appear even when the footer is turned off.

  • Alert trim: Mycelian is deleting old stored alerts (see Auto-trim stored alerts in App Settings). It shows Calculating, then N to delete, then N of M. Click it to open App Settings.
  • Copying / Deleting: a template is being duplicated or deleted. It shows Preparing, then N of M files. A Deleting badge is red.

Turn the footer on or off

  1. Connection status bar shows or hides the whole bar. The ? beside it opens this page.
  2. Badge style picks the full look. It is dimmed while Minimal badges is on.
  3. Minimal badges hides the names and shows the icons only. Hover its ? for a short reminder.
  4. Minimal badge style picks the minimal look. It is dimmed until Minimal badges is on.
  5. Changes take effect when you click Save.

Common questions

A service I set up has no badge

The badge appears once the credentials it needs are saved (see the table above). Open that service's Settings tab, check the fields are filled in, and click Save.

Twitch shows Idle even though I never use it

Twitch always has a badge. Gray Idle just means it is not set up. Nothing is wrong.

Everything turned red at once

Check the Internet badge first. When it is red, online services show No Internet or Unreachable until the network comes back. You get a single You're offline pop-up for this, not one per service. See Connection Problems.
