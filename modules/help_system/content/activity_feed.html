The Activity Feed lists every follow, sub, cheer, raid, tip and other event as it happens. It shows which alert is playing and which ones are next, lets you pause, mute and skip alerts, and replays a past alert with one click. The same feed runs as an OBS dock and stays in sync with the tab.

The Activity Feed tab

  1. Status pill. Playing · 2 up next while an alert plays, 2 up next between alerts, Ready when the queue is empty (Ready · muted when muted), and an amber Alerts paused · 2 waiting while paused.
  2. Pause holds new alerts until you click Resume; waiting alerts then play in order. Mute turns alert sound off (alerts still show) and becomes Unmute. Skip stops the alert that is playing now. The Pause button glows softly while an alert plays.
  3. {{icon:tune}} Feed look & storage opens SettingsApp Settings at the Activity Feed panel. {{icon:help_outline}} opens this page.
  4. Live is this session's events, History is every stored alert page by page, By person sums up each viewer. See Live, History and By person.
  5. In sync with the OBS dock: the open view, filters, search and history page are shared with the dock both ways. When the dock isn't open it reads OBS dock not open · copy link. Click it to copy the dock address.
  6. Search matches names, what happened, viewer messages and amounts. Filters picks which event types show; the badge shows how many are on, for example 8/11.
  7. Now playing strip: the playing alert, a countdown bar with the time left, and the next alerts as numbered Up next chips (+1 more when more are waiting). It shows on the Live view only.
  8. Alerts waiting to play stay in the list with a dashed border and an Up next · 1st, 2nd… tag in play order.
  9. The playing alert has sound bars, a green outline and its own Skip button.
  10. A played alert shows Click to replay when you point at it. Click anywhere on the row to replay it. The NEW tag marks events from the last 5 minutes.

Rows are newest first. Each one shows the event icon (with the sub tier on its corner), the viewer, what happened, the viewer's message, the amount, a Twitch or YouTube mark (tips get a Streamlabs or StreamElements mark), the time (just now, 3m, 2h) and an order number (#41, #42…) in the order alerts arrived. On the Live view, events older than 30 minutes move under Earlier. All of this can be changed in the settings.

Multi-month subs and gifts

When a viewer pays for several months at once, or gifts a sub that lasts several months, the row gets a tag after what happened. The tag counts the months bought at once and says what they are, so a resub that has run 14 months in total and was just paid 6 months ahead reads resubscribed · 14 months total with a 6 months paid ahead tag beside it. The tier stays on the icon corner (or after the name, with Tier badge set that way).

EventTagWhen the row is short on room

The tag always uses the full wording when it fits. Only a row whose text would otherwise be cut off (a narrow dock, a long name) switches to the short form, and it switches back when there is room again.

Multi-month tag style and placement

Pick how the tag looks with Multi-month tag style. Every layout (Rows, Cards and Ticker) and the OBS dock use the same style.

Multi-month tag placement puts the tag After the text (the default), on the Right side next to the amount, or Below the text on its own line.

  • The Ticker keeps one line per event, so Below the text shows the tag after the text there.
  • In the OBS dock's Rows layout, After the text puts the tag under the name, so the text isn't squeezed out by it.

Good to know:

  • Twitch reports the months for new subs, resubs and gifts to one viewer. A gift can show its tag a moment after the row appears, because Twitch sends the months separately from the gift.
  • A gift bomb (subs gifted to the community) shows the tag only when every sub in it has the same length; otherwise it shows none.
  • Places without a tag spell it out: the Now playing strip shows LongtimeFan resubscribed for 6 months · 14 months total, and By person shows Gifted 5 Tier 1 subs · 3 months each. Different lengths get separate By person lines.
  • Turn it all off with Show multi-month subs in the settings.

Paused alerts

While alerts are paused, an amber banner replaces the Now playing strip and lists the waiting alerts in the order they will play. Click Resume in the banner or in the header to play them.

With nothing waiting, the banner says New alerts wait in order below and play when you resume. Pausing also works from a Stream Deck Toggle Alerts key or a Source Controls button, and the feed follows.

Replay an alert

Click a played alert in the Live or History view to play it again. The tooltip says Click to replay this alert for the viewer, and a pop-up confirms, for example Replaying the Bits alert. If alerts are paused, the pop-up says the replay will play when you resume.

A replay…Details
keeps the original eventSame viewer, amount, message and details (reward title, months, gift count, raid size).
uses the alert as it is set up todayMedia, sound, duration, text-to-speech and other settings come from your current alert. Amounts are matched against your ranges again, so replaying a 500-bit cheer shows which alert 500 bits picks now.

Waiting and playing rows can't be replayed. Rows without a saved alert show This event has no saved alert, so it can't be replayed as their tooltip and don't react to clicks. If no alert is set up for that event type any more (for example you deleted your follow alert), Mycelian says This alert can't be replayed and asks you to set one up in the Alerts tab.

Replay is the quickest way to check alert ranges with real amounts. To test an alert you are editing, use Test Alert on the Alerts tab instead. See Testing Your Alerts.

Skip an alert

  • Skip in the header, or on the playing row, stops the playing alert straight away: the overlay hides it without its exit animation and stops its sound, speech and video. The next waiting alert starts, or the queue ends if nothing is waiting. While alerts are paused, nothing else starts.
  • One skip skips one alert. A quick double click, or a skip that arrives after the alert already ended, does not skip the next alert too. If nothing is playing, you get That alert isn't playing and nothing changes.
  • A skip sent with the id of a waiting alert (the skip_alert WebSocket event can carry an alert_id) removes that alert from the queue so it never plays.
  • Stream Deck keys, Spore Studio and Source Controls Skip current alert buttons and connector actions all skip the same way.

Layouts

Pick a layout in the settings. All three show the same events and the same playing and waiting states.

  • Rows (default): a timeline with a coloured dot per event and a line down the left. The playing dot is filled green and waiting dots are dashed amber.
  • Cards: a grid of cards that fills the width, with messages wrapped.
  • Ticker: one dense line per event in three blocks: Up next (plays in this order), Now playing, and Feed · newest first, where the latest event has a Newest tag. Fresh events get a dot after the name instead of the NEW tag.

The OBS dock

The dock is the same feed in a narrow panel inside OBS. It follows the Activity Feed settings and the app theme, and changes show in the open dock straight away.

Copy the dock address

On the Activity Feed tab, click OBS dock not open · copy link (or In sync with the OBS dock if one is already open). A pop-up confirms Activity Feed dock link copied. The address is http://127.0.0.1:5000/activity_feed; it is also listed under SettingsAbout.

Add a custom browser dock in OBS

In OBS, open Docks → Custom Browser Docks… (older versions: View → Docks). Name it, for example Activity Feed, paste the address as the URL and click Apply.

Use it while live

Pause, mute, skip, replay, search and filter from the dock. The tab shows In sync with the OBS dock while it is open.

  • The header is compact: Pause, Mute and Skip fill the width, with the view buttons and search on their own rows. Very narrow docks show the buttons as icons only.
  • Up next and Playing tags sit under the name.
  • A wide dock shows several columns only in the Cards layout and in By person; Rows and Ticker stay one column.
  • {{icon:tune}} in the dock pops up Feed look lives in Mycelian with Open in Mycelian, which opens the settings in the app.
  • If the dock loses Mycelian, the strip reads Offline · Reconnecting to Mycelian… and it reconnects on its own.
  • Pause, mute, skip and replay only work from a dock on the computer running Mycelian.

The dock's look is set only in SettingsApp SettingsActivity Feed. The activity_feed template in Source Settings has no settings of its own (it says This template has no settings). The dock's pop-ups are described in Notifications.

Troubleshooting

Replay says the alert can't be replayed or can't be found

The pop-up says why. Its saved details were cleaned up or couldn't be read from the database means the stored alert is gone: it was trimmed by the storage settings, or the database could not be read (check SettingsDatabase if it happens for recent alerts).

Alerts saved before this update used ids that could repeat within the same second, so an older alert may have been overwritten by another one saved at the same moment. Replaying such an alert can fail, or play the other event. Alerts saved from now on have unique ids and replay the right event.

A row doesn't react when I click it

Its tooltip says This event has no saved alert, so it can't be replayed. Some events have no saved record, for example raids and follows from before this update that had no alert set up (they are stored now). Waiting and playing rows can't be replayed either; use Skip on the playing one.

There's no alert set up for this kind of event any more

Replay uses your current alerts, and none matches this event. Set one up on the Alerts tab (check its range covers the amount), then replay again.

Skip does nothing

Nothing is playing (That alert isn't playing), or alerts are paused and the queue is waiting. Resume alerts first. If an alert stays on screen after Skip, refresh the alerts browser source in OBS. See Troubleshooting Alerts.

The Now playing strip or timer doesn't show

The strip shows only on the Live view, while an alert plays or waits, and when Show “Now playing” strip is on. A pulsing bar means the length is not known yet; keep the alerts browser source open in OBS. With Alert timer set to On each alert the countdown is on the playing row instead.

The dock looks different from the tab, or doesn't change

Both use the App Settings look. If the dock still shows an old design, right-click it in OBS and choose Refresh. If it shows a blank page because OBS started first, restart OBS with Mycelian running. See Setting Up Browser Sources in OBS.

History says Couldn't load stored alerts

The database could not be read. Check SettingsDatabase. If the feed is slow, lower the storage limits; see Performance.
