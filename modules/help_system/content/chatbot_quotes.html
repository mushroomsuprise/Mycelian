Save memorable lines from your stream and let chat pull them back up. Every quote has a number. Viewers use the built-in !quote and !add_quote commands; you manage the list on the Quotes tab.

The Quotes tab

  1. Search matches the quote, the author, who added it, or a number (4 or #4). The sort menu offers Newest first, Oldest first, By number and Most used. {{icon:refresh}} reloads; New quote opens the editor.
  2. The settings at a glance. The switch turns quotes on or off straight away. Then what viewers can type, the cooldown, who can use !add_quote, and how a reply looks, using your lowest-numbered quote. Quote settings changes them.
  3. The number, then — author or No author. {{icon:send}} Post this quote in chat now sends it with your reply format. If chat isn't connected, Mycelian says so and nothing is posted.
  4. How many times viewers called it up, or Never used. Then {{icon:content_copy}} Duplicate (saved as the next number), {{icon:edit}} Edit and {{icon:more_horiz}} with Copy the quote text and Delete….

With no quotes yet, the tab shows No quotes yet and an Add a quote button. Delete… asks Delete quote #4? and reminds you that its number stays free.

Add or edit a quote

  1. Click New quote.
  2. Type the line in Quote. Leave out the quotation marks; the reply format adds them.
  3. Optionally fill in Who said it and Added by. Left empty, Added by saves as UI.
  4. Check the preview, then click Add quote or press Ctrl+Enter.

Number starts at the next free number. You can change it, but it can't match another quote: the editor says #2 is already used by another quote.

Edit opens the same editor as Edit quote #2, showing when it was added and how often it was used. You can change the text, author, who added it and the number. It adds a Delete button; the save button reads Save.

Chat commands

These are built in while quotes are on. You don't create them on the Commands tab, and your own commands can't use the words quote or add_quote.

Type in chatBot replies
!quoteA random quote, as #2: "I meant to jump into the lava." — night_owl
!quote 2Quote number 2, or Quote #2 not found!
!quote lavaThe best match for lava in the text or author, or No quotes found matching 'lava'
!add_quote "text" authorSaves a new quote and replies Quote #5 added successfully! The author after the closing quotation mark is optional. Who added it is the chatter's name.

The bot stays silent when !quote is on cooldown, or when someone who isn't allowed tries !add_quote. !add_quote is for Mods by default; you, the broadcaster, can always use it.

Quote settings

  1. One cooldown shared by everyone, 30 seconds by default. It starts when a quote is posted in reply to !quote. 0 turns it off.
  2. Everyone, Subscribers (plus VIPs and mods), VIPs (plus mods) or Mods. The default is Mods.
  3. What the bot posts for !quote and for {{icon:send}} on a card. Type your own text; the { } button inserts the variables below, and the preview uses one of your quotes. Reset to default puts back #{number}: "{text}" — {author}; an empty box also saves as the default.
  4. Only shown when deleted quotes left gaps. Renumber numbers the quotes 1, 2, 3… in their current order. It happens at once and closes the dialog without saving your other changes, so save those first.
VariableBecomes
{number}The quote number
{text}The quote
{author}Who said it. When it is empty, — {author}, - {author} or by {author} is left out.
{date_added}When it was added, like Oct 14, 2026
{added_by}Who added it

Questions

Why did my quote numbers skip?

A new quote gets the highest number plus one, and deleting a quote doesn't renumber the others. To close the gaps, open Quote settings and click Renumber. Viewers who remember !quote 12 may get a different quote afterwards.

Can I post a quote from my own command?

Yes. Put {quote.random.formatted} in a command or event reply for a random quote with its author, or {quote.7.text} for quote number 7. The Variables panel lists them in its Quotes group. See Chatbot Variables.

!quote or !add_quote does nothing

Check that quotes are on and that Twitch is connected; quote commands only work in Twitch chat. The bot is also silent while the cooldown runs, and when the chatter isn't allowed to use !add_quote. Check Who can use !add_quote in Quote settings.

Someone added a quote I don't want

Find it with the search box or sort by Newest first, then use {{icon:more_horiz}} → Delete…. To stop it happening, set Who can use !add_quote to Mods.
