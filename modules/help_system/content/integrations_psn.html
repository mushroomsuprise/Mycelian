With PlayStation Network connected, Mycelian knows which PlayStation game you are playing and how many trophies you have, and shows it on stream with trophy overlays.

What the connection gives you

  • Now playing: the current game's name and cover art, from your PlayStation presence.
  • Trophy progress: earned and total trophies for the current game, and its completion percentage.
  • Trophy totals: your overall trophy counts by type, including platinums.
  • Online status: whether the tracked player is online.

Mycelian checks PlayStation presence about every 6 seconds and pushes changes to the overlays. PlayStation itself can take a few minutes to report a new trophy.

Trophies are not part of the alert system. There are no trophy alert types, and PSN has no chatbot variables. Trophy changes show up in the trophy overlays below.

Game mismatches and the game cache

PlayStation reports the game you are playing (presence) and your trophy lists separately, and the names do not always match. When Mycelian cannot find the presence name in your trophy data, a Game mismatch banner appears across the top of the PSN tab and the overlays may show no trophies.

  1. The banner names the presence name Mycelian could not match. Click Fix it in the game cache below to scroll to the cache.
  2. Every game Mycelian has seen is in the scrolling list, in name order. Type in Search cached games to filter by name, platform or ID; the heading then shows how many match. Only the first 150 matches are drawn, with "Showing 150 of … — type to narrow the list" under them.
  3. Click a game to open it in the panel on the right. The × on a row (Remove from cache) clears that entry; it is cached again the next time you play the game. With no games yet, the list shows "No cached games yet. Play a game to fill the cache."
  4. Set Presence name to the name in the banner (and the NP Title ID if you know it), then click Save game. The button stays disabled until you change a field. Save in the header also saves a pending game edit.

If the overlays look wrong

Trophies do not show for the game I am playing

Look for the Game mismatch banner on the PSN tab and fix the entry in the Game cache. Also check that trophy sync is on for the console, and give PlayStation a few minutes to report new trophies.

Nothing shows for another player

Check the PSN username to track spelling. The player must be your friend, appear online, and allow friends to see their trophies and online status.

My own game and trophies do not show

On the console, go to Settings → Users and Accounts → Privacy and let at least Friends see your trophies and online status.

The card says Token Expired

NPSSO tokens last about two months. Click Sign in again on the PSN tab to get a new one. See Connect PlayStation Network.

Security

  • Mycelian does not store your PlayStation password. The sign-in window keeps only the NPSSO token.
  • The NPSSO token is stored in Mycelian's database on this computer. Treat it like a password and do not show it on stream.
  • Overlays never receive the token.
  • Signing out of PlayStation on all devices ends the token.
