The Statistics tab shows what happened on your stream: how much, when, and who. Pick a date range once and every chart, number and leaderboard follows it.

The date range bar

  1. Range. Presets or Custom (pick two dates, then Apply). Every range except All time is compared with the equally long period right before it.
  2. Live keeps the numbers current: lifetime totals every 5 seconds, the event log every 30 seconds when the range includes today. Refresh reloads now. Save writes lifetime totals immediately (they also save on their own).

Sections

  • Tiles show a total for the range, the change versus the previous period (▲/▼ with a percentage; New when there was nothing before), and a small trend line. Click a tile to chart it.
  • Over time charts the selected number per hour (Today), per day, or per week (ranges over about six months). Switch between columns and a line, and turn on Previous period average for a reference line. Hover for exact values.
  • Spotlight names the most active viewer, top chatter, biggest bit supporter, top gifter and longest watch streak, with their Twitch profile picture when Mycelian knows they are a Twitch viewer (otherwise their initial). Click a name to open that viewer.
  • What happened ranks every kind of event in the range. Click a row to chart it.
  • When your community is active is a weekday × hour heatmap in your local time, for chat messages or all events.

Top 10 viewers for chat, bits, gifted subs, channel points, subs, watch streaks, raids, giveaway entries and bot commands. Bars compare each viewer to the leader. Click a name to open their details. The system account Mycelian uses for polls and giveaway draws is never ranked.

All-time totals that Mycelian keeps from the day you started: alerts, chat, hype train completions by level, chatbot, connectors, quotes, giveaways, polls and counters your overlays report. These do not follow the date range and update live.

Type a username (suggestions come from everyone with recorded activity) and press Enter or Look up. You get first and last seen, their numbers for the range with changes, an activity chart, their lifetime totals, and a timeline of recent events you can filter by type.

Ranges come from the event log, which records each alert, chat message, command, connector run and giveaway entry with its time. Events from before Mycelian started keeping that log only appear in Lifetime.

Export highlights

The Export highlights section builds a shareable image for the selected range, using your theme's colors. It shows a preview before you save.

Pick the range

Use the date range bar at the top, for example 7 days for a weekly recap.

Choose a layout

Wide 16:9 (1920×1080) fits X, Discord and YouTube community posts. Portrait 4:5 (1080×1350) fits Instagram and other phone-first feeds.

Save PNG

The image is saved to the exports folder next to your Mycelian data as highlights_<start>_<end>.png. The folder button opens it.

The image includes your channel name (from the connected Twitch account), active viewers, chat messages, bits, subs and gifted subs with changes versus the previous period, daily activity with the peak day, an MVP, and top chatters, cheerers, gifters and channel point spenders.

Questions

Why is a range empty when Lifetime shows numbers?

Lifetime totals go back further than the event log. The empty-range notice tells you which dates the log covers; All time shows everything it has.

Why does Active viewers not add up across days?

It counts distinct people. Someone who chats on three days counts once for the range and once on each day.

Can I reset statistics here?

No. This tab only reads statistics; nothing on it changes or deletes stored data.
