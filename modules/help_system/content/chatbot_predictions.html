The Predictions tab

Pick the overlay

Each overlay with a Prediction widget keeps its own question and outcomes. Mycelian ships a ready-made predictions overlay; add it to OBS from SettingsAbout or the Overlay card.

Write the question and outcomes

Up to 45 characters for the question and 2 to 10 outcomes of up to 25 characters. On Twitch the first outcome is blue and the others are pink.

Start it

Click Start prediction. Bets stay open for the Betting time (30 seconds to 30 minutes), or click Lock bets to close them early.

Pay out or refund

When you know the result, click Won on the winning outcome. Cancel & refund gives everyone their points back.

When it ends

Mycelian can post the result in Twitch chat and show it on the chat banner. The message can use {title}, {winner}, {points}, {winners} (predictors who won), {users} and {top} (the biggest winner). A separate message is used when the prediction is canceled.

Only Twitch partners and affiliates can run predictions, and a channel runs one at a time. A prediction started from the Twitch dashboard also shows here and on the overlay. If the tab asks you to sign in again, open SettingsTwitch and click Connect.

Connectors can start, lock, resolve and cancel predictions, and can react when one begins, locks or ends. See Connector actions.
