PlayStation Network has no app keys to create. Mycelian signs in with your NPSSO token, a 64-character code Sony gives your browser after you sign in. Connect to PSN captures it for you.

After you connect, see PlayStation Network Integration for the trophy overlays and the game cache.

Before you start

  • You know the email and password of your PlayStation Network account, and have your phone ready if two-step verification is on.
  • On your console, trophies and online status are visible to at least Friends (Settings → Users and Accounts → Privacy). Mycelian can only see what your privacy settings allow.

Connect your account

Click Connect to PSN

Open SettingsPSN and click Connect to PSN.

  1. Leave PSN username to track empty to track your own account. It is a plain text field; only the NPSSO code is masked.
  2. Connect to PSN starts the automatic sign-in. How to connect shows the manual steps. Once a code is saved, the button reads Sign in again.

Read the steps and click Continue

A dialog explains what happens next. Continue unlocks after a 10-second countdown.

Sign in to PlayStation

A window titled Mycelian — PlayStation sign-in opens on playstation.com. Click Sign In, enter your email and password, and finish two-step verification if asked. Wait until the page shows you signed in.

Use the NPSSO menu

With the sign-in window in front, open the NPSSO menu and click I am signed in — retrieve NPSSO token. On macOS the menu is in the menu bar at the top of the screen; on Windows it is at the top of the window.

  1. This menu only exists in the Mycelian sign-in window. If you close the window before using it, nothing is saved; click Connect to PSN (or Sign in again) again.

Check it worked

Mycelian shows "Connecting to PlayStation", then "Signed in to PlayStation". It fills in NPSSO code, saves it, and connects. You do not need to click Save or restart. After a few seconds:

When you are not online on a console, the pill and the card show Offline in amber. That still means the sign-in worked.

Use this if the sign-in window does not work on your computer. It is the same as How to connect on the PSN tab.

  1. In your normal browser, sign in at www.playstation.com.
  2. In the same browser, open https://ca.account.sony.com/api/v1/ssocookie. The page shows one line of text:
  3. On SettingsPSN, open NPSSO code on the account card and paste it into the field.
  4. Optional: enter a PSN username to track to follow someone else (see below).
  5. Click Save in the tab's header. Mycelian reconnects PSN with the new token.

The NPSSO token gives full access to your PlayStation account. Do not show it on stream or share it.

Track another player

Put a PSN online ID in PSN username to track (under Tracking, on the right) and click Save to show that player's game and trophies instead of yours. You can only track your own profile or friends who have their status set to online. The card then reads Connected · Tracking name, and its Tracking cell shows name with tracking target under it.

If something goes wrong

"Window closed before NPSSO was retrieved"

The sign-in window closed before you used NPSSO → I am signed in — retrieve NPSSO token. Click Connect to PSN (or Sign in again) and use the menu while the window is still open.

"PlayStation sign-in didn't finish" or "NPSSO could not be retrieved"

You were not fully signed in when you used the menu. Finish signing in on playstation.com, then use the menu again. If it keeps failing, use Paste the token by hand above.

The card says Token Expired

NPSSO tokens last about two months, and signing out of PlayStation everywhere ends them early. Mycelian shows "PlayStation sign-in expired". Click Sign in again on the PSN tab to get a new one.

The card says Token Expired right after pasting a token

Mycelian reports any token Sony refuses as Token Expired, including a mistyped one. Copy only the 64 characters inside the quotes, with no spaces or braces, or use Sign in again instead.

The card says Authentication Failed

Sony answered with an error other than a bad token, for example a blocked request. Wait a few minutes and click Save in the header to reconnect. The log has the details.

Connected, but no game or trophies show

Check the console privacy settings from Before you start. When tracking another player, they must be your friend and appear online. PlayStation can take a few minutes to report new trophies.
