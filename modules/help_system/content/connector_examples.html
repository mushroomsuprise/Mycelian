Complete connectors you can rebuild in a few minutes. Each tab shows the finished card, the values to enter in the editor, and what happens on stream.

New to connectors? Read Connector System Introduction first. Values in the tables are typed or picked in the editor exactly as shown: Trigger in When, conditions as Field / Is / Value in If, and actions in Then.

Examples

TriggerRaid (Twitch)
ConditionViewers · at least (≥) · 10
Action 1Send chat message, Send to Twitch only:
Welcome raiders from {username}! All {viewer_count} of you, make yourselves at home.
Action 2OBS action, OBS — Set program scene, Scene Raid Welcome. Wait 2 seconds.
Action 3Duplicate action 2, change Scene to Main, wait 10 seconds.

What happens: a raid with 10 or more viewers posts the welcome, waits 2 seconds, shows your Raid Welcome scene for 10 seconds, then returns to Main. Smaller raids do nothing. Change the scene names to ones in your OBS.

TriggerChat message (Twitch)
Condition 1Message · first word is · !so
Condition 2AND · Is a moderator · is · true
Action 1Send chat message, Send to Twitch only:
Go follow {message_after_word.1} at twitch.tv/{message_after_word.1}

What happens: a moderator (or you) types !so CoolStreamer. The first word is condition matches !so and removes it, so {message_after_word.1} is CoolStreamer. The bot posts Go follow CoolStreamer at twitch.tv/CoolStreamer. Viewers who are not moderators get no reply.

TriggerChat message (Twitch)
ConditionMessage · first word is · !lurk, Aa on
Action 1Send chat message, Send to Twitch only:
Enjoy the lurk, {username}! See you when you're back.

What happens: anyone who types !lurk (or !lurk brb dinner) gets a reply with their name. With Aa on, !Lurk matches too. To answer YouTube viewers as well, duplicate it, switch the trigger to YouTube Chat message and set Send to to YouTube. For plain text replies like this one, chatbot commands also work.

TriggerBits cheered
ConditionBits · at least (≥) · 500
Action 1Show a banner: {username} cheered {amount} bits!, Show for 15 sec
Action 2Send announcement, Colour Purple: Huge thanks to {username} for {amount} bits!

What happens: cheers of 500 bits or more show a banner on your chat overlay for 15 seconds and post a purple announcement in Twitch chat. Your normal bits alert still plays as usual. Announcements need the chatbot to have the announcements permission.

For tiers (100–499, 500+…), make one connector per range, for example Bits at least (≥) 100 AND Bits less than (<) 500 on the first, Bits at least (≥) 500 on the second.

TriggerNew subscriber
ConditionTier (1-3) · is · 3
Action 1Send announcement, Colour Orange: {username} just subscribed at Tier 3. Thank you!

What happens: a new Tier 3 subscription gets an orange announcement. Tiers are 1, 2 or 3 (Prime counts as 1). Returning subscribers who share a resub message use the separate Resubscription trigger; duplicate this connector and click Change on the trigger to cover them.

TriggerChannel points reward
ConditionReward name · is · Hydrate (the reward's title on Twitch)
Action 1Show a banner: {username} says: drink some water!, Show for 20 sec
Action 2Write to a file, File logs/hydrate.log, Add to the end instead of replacing on, Text {username} redeemed Hydrate at {time} followed by a new line

What happens: each redemption of the Hydrate reward shows a banner for 20 seconds and adds a line to logs/hydrate.log in the Mycelian data folder. Other rewards do nothing. If the reward asks viewers for text, {user_input} holds it.

TriggerHotkey, Keys ctrl+shift+b (or click Record and press them)
Action 1OBS action, OBS — Set program scene, Scene BRB
Action 2OBS action, OBS — Set input mute, Input Mic/Aux, Muted Muted

What happens: pressing Ctrl+Shift+B, even while your game has focus, switches OBS to BRB and mutes the mic. Make a second connector (for example ctrl+shift+n) that switches back and unmutes. Using a Stream Deck button trigger instead puts the same connector on a button.

TriggerSuper Chat (YouTube)
ConditionAmount · at least (≥) · 5
Action 1Send chat message, Send to YouTube only:
Thank you {username} for the {display_amount} Super Chat!

What happens: Super Chats of 5 or more (in the sender's currency) get a thank-you in YouTube chat, with the amount as YouTube shows it, such as $5.00. Compare on Amount; Amount as shown is text and does not work with at least (≥).

TriggerTip / donation
ConditionAmount · at least (≥) · 20
Action 1Send Discord message: {username} just tipped {formatted_amount} on stream: {message}. Under Channels pick your server and #stream-chat, then Add

What happens: Streamlabs, StreamElements and YouTube Super Chat tips of 20 or more (in your alert currency) are posted to your Discord channel with the tipper's message. Add Source is not youtube to leave Super Chats out. Needs your Discord bot connected; see Connect Discord.

Tips for your own connectors

  • Start small. One trigger and one action. Test, then add.
  • Test sends for real. {{icon:play_arrow}} on the card and Save & test run the actions with sample data (100 bits, a raid of 42…), so a chat action posts to your real chat. If your conditions reject the sample, nothing runs; see Testing a connector.
  • Watch for loops. A chat connector with Message contains hype whose reply also contains "hype" can answer itself. Use first word is with a !command, keep the trigger word out of the reply, or set a Cooldown.
  • Use waits for overlays. Give a scene or banner a few seconds before the next step.
  • Plan for missing data. An anonymous cheer or an empty message leaves its variable blank. Write messages that still read well without it; the preview line in the editor helps.
  • Need OR? Click the AND/OR pill on a condition row. The summary line under the conditions says exactly when the connector runs.
