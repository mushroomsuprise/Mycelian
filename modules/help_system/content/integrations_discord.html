With your own Discord bot connected, Mycelian can post to the Discord channels you choose: automatic go-live announcements, Chatbot replies and Connector messages.

The bot uses a bot token, never your Discord password. The token is stored encrypted in the app database. It sends messages only, so privileged intents such as Server Members or Message Content are not needed.

Go-live announcements

Under Go-live announcement on SettingsDiscord, turn on Announce when you go live, add one or more channels, and click Save in the header. Mycelian then posts your message the first time Twitch or YouTube goes live in a session.

PlaceholderBecomes
{platform}Twitch or YouTube, whichever went live first
{title}For YouTube, the latest video title Mycelian knows. Twitch go-live posts leave it empty
{url}Your Twitch channel link or YouTube live link

The default message is Stream is live on {platform}! {url}.

Multi-streaming sends only one message. When the second platform goes live, Discord is not posted again. The session resets once both Twitch and YouTube are offline, so the next stream announces again.

Chatbot and Connectors

The servers and channels your bot can see also appear in these places once the bot is connected:

WhereHow
Chatbot commands and eventsIn the reply step, Discord → Channel adds Discord channels for the reply. It is separate from Send to Twitch / YouTube, so a reply can go to Discord only. See Chatbot Commands.
ConnectorsUse the Send Discord Message action, not the Twitch/YouTube chat action. See Connector Actions.

Status

Discord appears in the status footer once a bot token is saved. The status colors and fixes are in Connect Discord; the full legend is in Status Footer.

Troubleshooting

The tab says Auth failed

Reset the bot token in the Developer Portal, paste it, and click Connect.

No servers are listed

Invite the bot to at least one server, then click the {{icon:refresh}} icon next to Add (Reload servers and channels). The Server and Channel pickers only show while the bot is connected.

Messages are not sent

Make sure the bot's role can View Channel and Send Messages in that channel.
