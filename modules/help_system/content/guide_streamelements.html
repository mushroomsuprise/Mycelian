Mycelian listens for StreamElements tips using your channel's JWT token. You copy it from the StreamElements dashboard and paste it into Mycelian. There is no app to register.

{{icon:schedule}} About 3 minutes {{icon:person}} The StreamElements account that receives tips {{icon:place}} SettingsStreamElements

Only tips come from StreamElements. Follows and subscriptions stay on the Twitch connection. What happens with tips once they arrive is in StreamElements Integration.

Before you start

  • You can sign in at the StreamElements dashboard.
  • You know which linked channel receives tips (Twitch, YouTube or Kick). Each channel has its own JWT.

Connect StreamElements

Select the channel that receives tips

Sign in to the StreamElements dashboard. If more than one platform is linked, click your avatar in the top-right and choose the channel that receives tips.

Copy the JWT token

Open Account → Channels. Turn on Show secrets, then copy the JWT Token.

  1. The JWT is hidden until Show secrets is on.
  2. Copy the JWT Token, not the Account ID. A JWT is long and starts with eyJ.

The JWT gives full access to your StreamElements channel. Keep it private. Mycelian stores it encrypted.

StreamElements explains this page in How to locate your Account ID and JWT token.

Paste it into Mycelian and click Connect

Open SettingsStreamElements. Pick your Alert currency, paste the token into JWT token, and click Connect.

  1. Alert currency is shared with Streamlabs. Donation alert thresholds use it.
  2. The field is masked. Click the eye icon to check what you pasted.
  3. Connect saves the token and opens the connection. The status shows Connecting, then Connected; there is no pop-up when it works, and a notification says why if it can't connect. Disconnect also deletes the saved token.

If the field is empty you will see "Paste a StreamElements JWT first."

Check it worked

Within a few seconds the status turns green:

To test, use Emulate → tip in your StreamElements overlay editor. Emulated tips reach Mycelian too and play a donation alert.

If something goes wrong

"Authentication failed: StreamElements rejected the JWT."

The token is wrong, incomplete, or was regenerated in the dashboard. Copy the JWT Token again (not the Account ID), paste it, and click Connect.

"Token expired: Paste a new JWT from the StreamElements dashboard."

JWTs have an expiry date. Mycelian checks it and also shows a notification when it has passed. Copy a fresh token from Account → Channels and connect again.

"Error: Could not read the channel id from the JWT."

What you pasted is not a channel JWT, or StreamElements could not be reached to look up the channel. Check the token, then check your internet connection. Mycelian keeps retrying in the background.

Connected, but tips do not alert

You may have copied the JWT of a different linked channel. Switch to the right channel with the avatar menu and copy its JWT. Also check that donation alerts are set up in Alerts → Donations.

A tip alerted late, or not at all

Tips held for moderation alert only after you allow them. Rejected tips are ignored.
