With Steam connected, Mycelian knows which Steam game you are playing and how many of its achievements you have, and shows it on stream with the Steam achievements overlay.

What the connection gives you

  • Profile: your Steam name, profile picture, status (Online, Away, Busy...) and Steam level.
  • Now playing: the current game's name and artwork.
  • Achievements: unlocked and total achievements for the current game, its completion percentage, every achievement with its icon and how many players have it, and the ones you unlocked most recently.

Steam achievements overlay

The steam_achievements template looks and behaves like the PlayStation trophies overlay: game art, the game name, "X out of Y achievements", your Steam level, a progress bar with a shine, a gold badge once every achievement is unlocked, and a "+1 achievement" pop-up when you unlock one. It fades out after 30 seconds without a game.

  1. Open SettingsAbout and find steam_achievements under Browser source URLs, then add it to OBS as a browser source (470 × 230).
  2. Change colors, fonts and sizes in Source Settings, or open it in Spore Studio to move things around.
  3. Behavior (update interval, auto-hide, the pop-up, the badge and the text formats) is in the STEAM_SETTINGS block at the top of the template's Advanced JS.

Build your own

Spore Studio offers two Steam events for data feeds and Advanced JS:

EventWhenUseful fields
steam_data_updateAbout every 15 secondspersona_name, avatar_url, current_game_name, current_game_art_url, achievement_counts.unlocked, achievement_counts.total, current_game_progress, steam_level, last_unlock.name
steam_achievement_unlockedOnce per new achievementname, description, icon_url, global_percent, game_name

An overlay can ask for the latest data at any time by emitting get_steam_data. Feeds listening to a Steam event ask for it on their own when the overlay loads.

Steam achievements are not part of the alert system: there are no achievement alert types and no chatbot variables.
