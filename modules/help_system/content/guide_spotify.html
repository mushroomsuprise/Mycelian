Mycelian reads what Spotify is playing through your own Spotify app. You create it once in the Spotify Developer Dashboard, paste its Client ID and Client Secret into Mycelian, then sign in.

{{icon:schedule}} About 5 minutes {{icon:person}} Spotify Premium account {{icon:place}} SettingsSpotify

After you connect, see Spotify Integration for the overlay and what it shows.

Before you start

  • You have a Spotify Premium subscription.
  • You can sign in to the Spotify Developer Dashboard with the Spotify account you want to show on stream.
  • Mycelian is open on this computer. Sign-in returns to http://127.0.0.1:9973, which only works on the machine running Mycelian.

Connect your account

Create a Spotify app

Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard, sign in, and click Create app. Fill in the form like this:

  1. Type http://127.0.0.1:9973 and click Add. It must be http, 127.0.0.1 (not localhost), port 9973, and no trailing slash.
  2. Tick Web API and Web Playback SDK.

Copy the Client ID and Client Secret

Open the new app and go to Settings → Basic Information.

  1. Copy the Client ID.
  2. Click View client secret and copy the Client secret.

Treat the Client Secret like a password. If it leaks, rotate it on the same page and paste the new value into Mycelian.

Paste both values into Mycelian

Open SettingsSpotify, expand Advanced and paste the two values.

  1. Advanced is collapsed by default. Both fields are masked; the eye icon shows the value.
  2. The copy button copies the redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:9973. Connect saves the Client ID and Client Secret, then opens your browser. Refresh only updates the status labels.

Click Connect and approve access

Mycelian opens Spotify's authorization page in your browser. Sign in and click Agree. Mycelian asks for two permissions: read your playback state and read your currently playing track. It cannot control playback or change your library.

The browser then shows "Authorization Successful!". Close the tab and return to Mycelian.

Mycelian waits 5 minutes for you to approve. After that the status shows Authorization Timeout; click Connect again. If Advanced is empty you get "Please enter your Spotify Client ID and Client Secret first!" instead.

Check it worked

Start a song in any Spotify app (desktop, web player or phone). Within a few seconds the tab should look like this:

Click Test any time to check the saved token. It shows "Spotify connection successful!" or asks you to check your credentials. Mycelian refreshes the token on its own.

Market country

Market country sets the region used for Spotify API requests. Leave it on Auto (from account) unless tracks show up as unavailable. The other choices are US, GB and CA. Click Save after changing it.

If something goes wrong

Spotify says "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI"

The redirect URI in the dashboard does not match exactly. Open your app's Settings, make sure http://127.0.0.1:9973 is listed under Redirect URIs, save, and click Connect again.

Status shows "Server Error"

Mycelian could not open port 9973 for the sign-in callback. Another program, or a second copy of Mycelian, is using it. Close it and try again.

Authorization fails or the account is rejected

Clear cookies for spotify.com, or try a different browser. If you sign in with a different Spotify account than the one that owns the app, add that account under User Management in the app's settings.

Current track says "Spotify connected (playback may be restricted by region)"

Mycelian is signed in but Spotify reports nothing playing. Start playback, check that Spotify is not in offline mode, and make sure the right device is active. If it persists, try a specific Market country.

Status says Expired

The saved token could not be refreshed, usually because the Client Secret was rotated or access was removed in your Spotify account under Manage apps. Paste the current secret and click Connect.
