What a donation does

Each Streamlabs donation:

  • plays the matching alert from Alerts → Donations,
  • appears in the Activity Feed,
  • fires donation events for the Chatbot and Connectors.

The same donation is never alerted twice, even if it arrives both on the live socket and in Mycelian's donation check.

Alert currency

Alert currency, under Donations on this tab, is the currency your donation thresholds in Alerts → Donations are written in. It is shared with StreamElements.

  1. A tip in another currency is converted to the alert currency using daily exchange rates (Frankfurter / ECB), cached for an hour.
  2. The converted amount is rounded to the nearest whole unit (never below 1) to pick the alert.
  3. The alert shows the original amount first and the converted amount in parentheses.

If no exchange rate can be fetched, Mycelian matches the donor's original amount as-is.

Status

Streamlabs appears in the status footer once a Client ID is saved. The status colors and fixes are in Connect Streamlabs; the full legend is in Status Footer.

The {{icon:logout}} Disconnect icon on the account card stops the connection and deletes the saved sign-in. Your Client ID and Client Secret stay, so Connect signs you in again.
