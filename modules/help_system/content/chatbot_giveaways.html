Run a chat giveaway from Chatbot → Giveaways. Viewers enter by typing a keyword you choose. You draw winners, and the chatbot posts the result as a Twitch chat announcement. Everything on the tab saves as you change it.

{{icon:schedule}} About 4 minutes {{icon:place}} ChatbotGiveaways {{brand:twitch}} Twitch chat

Announcements need the chatbot account to be a moderator with the moderator:manage:announcements permission. Setting up the chatbot account is covered in Twitch Setup.

The Giveaways tab

  1. The status, with the keyword, how long ago entries opened, how many win per draw and who can enter. If something went wrong, such as a failed announcement, an amber line under the status says what happened.
  2. The buttons change with the status, as shown under The three states below.
  3. Everyone in the pool, newest first. The badge counts entries. {{icon:content_copy}} copies every entrant's name, one per line.
  4. Hover an entrant to see {{icon:close}} Remove from the pool and {{icon:block}} Remove and block from future giveaways.
  5. The keyword, how many win per draw and the winner announcement.
  6. Who can enter and how draws work. Defaults shown.
  7. The giveaway overlay for OBS.

The three states

The box at the top shows where the giveaway is and offers the next steps.

Start giveaway opens entries. Without a keyword it says "Set an entry keyword before starting." and puts the cursor in the keyword box.

Stop entries closes entries; the pool stays so you can draw. Draw winner (Draw 3 winners when you draw more than one) works while entries are still open, and is greyed out while the pool is empty.

Reopen entries lets chat enter again with the same pool.

After a draw the status names the winners and when the announcement went out, or says Drawn at 20:14, but the chat announcement failed. Draw again (Draw 3 more for several winners) draws from what is left in the pool.

New giveaway asks first: "This empties the pool (5 entrants), clears the keyword and forgets the last draw. The overlay clears too." Click New giveaway again to confirm, or Cancel. Then set a keyword for the next one.

Run a giveaway

Set the keyword

In Setup, type what viewers must send in Entry keyword, for example !enter. It saves when you press Enter or click away. The whole message must equal the keyword. Capitals do not matter and spaces at the ends are ignored, but "!enter please" does not count.

Start

Click Start giveaway. The status turns green: Accepting entries.

Let chat enter

Entrants appear on the tab as they type the keyword.

Close entries

Click Stop entries when you're ready. You can also draw while entries are still open.

Draw

Click Draw winner. Mycelian picks up to Winners per draw names and posts one announcement.

With Remove winners after a draw on (the default), winners leave the pool, so Draw again picks a runner-up.

Finish

When you're done, click New giveaway to empty the pool and clear the keyword.

Entrants

  1. A trophy marks winners of the last draw who are still in the pool (when Remove winners after a draw is off). They are listed first.
  2. With One entry per viewer off, each message is another entry. The badge shows how many a viewer has.
  3. How long ago they entered. Hover for the exact time and the remove buttons. Removing takes all of that viewer's entries out of the pool. Remove and block also adds them to Blocked viewers.

Find a viewer filters by name. The list shows the first 400 entrants; past that it says how many more there are, so search to find someone.

Last draw

After a draw this card appears at the top of the right column. It lists the winners and says whether the announcement went out (announced in chat, chat and the banner, or the chat announcement didn't send).

Announce again posts the announcement once more, and the banner too if that is on. Copy names copies the winners, comma separated.

Setup

SettingWhat it does
Entry keywordThe exact message viewers send to enter, in any letter case. Changing it while entries are open tells the overlay the new keyword; entries made with the old one stay. Emptying it while entries are open closes entries.
Winners per drawHow many names one draw picks, 1 to 100. Use − and +.
Winner announcementThe message posted after a draw. The preview below it uses sample names, or the real winners after a draw.
Also show on the chat bannerAlso shows the announcement on the chat overlay banner. The chat announcement is still sent. Off by default.

The announcement is sent as a Twitch announcement, the highlighted chat message, not a normal line. The { } button in the box inserts these variables:

VariableBecomes
{winners}The winners' names, comma separated
{winner}The same as {winners}
{count}How many won
 
🎉 {count} winners: {winners}. Thanks for entering!

The default is Congratulations {winners}!. Leave the box empty and the default comes back. With a dedicated chatbot account the announcement comes from the bot. In fallback mode (no separate bot) it comes from your main account.

Rules

RuleDefaultWhat it does
One entry per viewerOnOne entry per Twitch account. Off lets a viewer enter many times, and each entry is another chance to win.
Can't win twice in one drawOnWhen a draw picks more than one winner, each is a different viewer. Off can pick the same viewer twice if they have several entries.
Remove winners after a drawOnTakes every entry of each winner out of the pool, so Draw again picks someone new.
Moderators can't enterOffChatters with the moderator badge cannot enter.
VIPs can't enterOffChatters with the VIP badge cannot enter.

Blocked viewers can never enter. Type a name and press Enter (or click away) to add it; a leading @ is dropped. Click {{icon:close}} on a name to unblock it. Bots like nightbot are worth adding.

Overlay

The built-in giveaway overlay template shows the keyword, entrants and winners on stream. In the Overlay card:

  • Click the address to copy it, then add it to OBS as a Browser Source.
  • Customize look opens Source Settings with the giveaway template selected. See Configuring Template Settings.
  • Preview opens the overlay in your browser.

Refreshing the browser source keeps the current keyword and entrants. Removing an entrant on the tab updates the overlay's list, and New giveaway clears the overlay. See Templates.

Statistics

Mycelian counts giveaways completed (each draw), total entries, average entries per giveaway and how often each name has won. The totals are on SettingsStatistics.

Troubleshooting

Nobody gets entered
  • The status must say Accepting entries. Click Start giveaway or Reopen entries.
  • The message must be exactly the keyword, nothing more.
  • The viewer may be blocked, a moderator or VIP with that rule on, or already entered.
  • If the keyword is the name of one of your commands, the command answers and the message is not an entry. Pick a keyword that is not a command.
  • Only Twitch chat can enter.
Winners are drawn but no announcement shows

The toast says "Drew winner(s) but announcement failed." and an amber line under the status explains. Check that the chatbot is connected and is a moderator on your channel, then click Announce again on the Last draw card. The draw still counts, and the winners are still removed if that rule is on.

The same person keeps winning

Turn on Remove winners after a draw, or click New giveaway between rounds.
