Mycelian reads Streamlabs donations through your own Streamlabs app. You register the app once, paste its Client ID and Client Secret into Mycelian, then sign in.

{{icon:schedule}} About 10 minutes {{icon:person}} The Streamlabs account that receives donations {{icon:place}} SettingsStreamlabs

Only donations come from Streamlabs. Follows, subscriptions and bits stay on the Twitch connection. What happens with donations once they arrive is in Streamlabs Integration.

Before you start

  • You can sign in at streamlabs.com/login with the account that receives donations.
  • You know your Streamlabs username. You will add it to the app's whitelist.
  • Mycelian is open on this computer. The sign-in returns to http://127.0.0.1:9975, which only works on the machine running Mycelian.

Connect Streamlabs

Register an application

Open Streamlabs apps and register a new app. Streamlabs' own steps are in Register your application. Fill it in like this:

  1. Paste http://127.0.0.1:9975 exactly: http, 127.0.0.1 (not localhost), port 9975, no trailing slash. A mismatch is why Connect lands on an error page.
  2. Until Streamlabs approves an app, only whitelisted users can authorize it. Add the account that receives donations. You do not need to submit the app for public review.

Copy the Client ID and Client Secret

Open the app you just registered. Copy its Client ID and Client Secret.

Treat the Client Secret like a password. Mycelian stores it encrypted.

Paste both values into Mycelian

Open SettingsStreamlabs. Paste the two values under Client ID & secret on the account card, pick your Alert currency on the right, and click Connect.

  1. How to connect opens a short version of these steps with the redirect URI and a copy button. The redirect URI is also in the copy box under Client ID & secret.
  2. Alert currency is the currency your donation alert thresholds use. It is shared with StreamElements.
  3. Connect saves the fields first, then opens your browser. If either field is empty you see "Enter your Streamlabs Client ID and Client Secret first". Only the Client secret is masked.

Approve access in the browser

Streamlabs asks you to approve the app. Sign in as the donation account and approve the request. Mycelian asks for two scopes only:

ScopeUsed for
donations.readReading donations
socket.tokenOpening the live donation socket

See Streamlabs' scope list. Mycelian waits up to 3 minutes for you to approve. After that, click Connect again.

Check it worked

While you approve, a Connecting to Streamlabs pop-up waits and the button reads Connecting…. It turns into Connected to Streamlabs and the card turns green:

Mycelian refreshes the Streamlabs token on its own and reconnects when the app starts. The next donation shows in the Activity Feed and plays a donation alert.

Connection status

The Streamlabs tab and the bottom status bar show these states. On the tab, the state is the name on the account card and the header pill; any extra detail is the card's state line, for example Reconnecting to Streamlabs under Connecting. The full legend is in Status Footer.

If something goes wrong

Connect opens an error page on Streamlabs

The app's redirect URI does not match. Set it to http://127.0.0.1:9975 exactly, save the app, and click Connect again.

Streamlabs says I am not allowed to authorize the app

Your username is not on the app's whitelist. Add the account that receives donations and try again.

"Another app is using the port Streamlabs sign-in needs (9975)"

Another program on this computer is listening on port 9975. Close it (often another copy of Mycelian or another streaming tool) and click Connect again.

"Streamlabs sign-in timed out"

The browser did not come back within 3 minutes, or the window was closed before approving. Click Connect and approve the prompt.

"Couldn't connect to Streamlabs" with "Streamlabs rejected the authorization code." in the details

The Client Secret is wrong or belongs to another app. Paste both values again from the same app and connect.

The card says Token expired

The saved token could not be refreshed. Click Connect to sign in again.
