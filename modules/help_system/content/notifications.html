Mycelian tells you what it is doing with small pop-ups in the corner of the window. Every pop-up is also kept in the notification centre behind the bell, so you can read it later. You choose what pops up, for how long, and what it looks like.

Reading a pop-up

The colored icon and the thin accent show the level at a glance. The rest of the card is plain text.

  1. Level and feature. Error, Warning, Info, Done or Working, then the part of Mycelian it came from. A ×2, ×3… tag appears here when the same thing happened again (see below).
  2. Title and message. What happened, in one line, with detail under it.
  3. How to fix it. Errors and warnings say what to do next.
  4. Buttons. For example Open Twitch settings, Copy details (copies the technical text for a bug report) or Try again.

Hover, click and close

  • Pop-ups with a timer show a thin bar along the bottom that shrinks until the pop-up closes. Hover the pop-up to pause it; move away and it carries on.
  • If a pop-up has an Open … button, clicking anywhere on the card does the same thing.
  • Click {{icon:close}} to close it. It stays in the notification centre.
  • When more pop-ups arrive than fit (see Most on screen at once), the oldest close first. Errors and work in progress are kept longest.

Progress notices

Some actions take a few seconds, such as testing a connection. Mycelian shows a Working pop-up while it runs, then turns that same pop-up into the result. You get one pop-up, not two. Click the tabs to see each stage.

×N: when the same thing happens again

If the same notification fires again shortly after the first one, Mycelian does not stack a new pop-up. It updates the one you already have, adds a ×2, ×3… tag after the feature name and restarts its timer. In the notification centre the entry says · 3 times. A burst of identical errors therefore stays one line, and the count tells you how often it happened.

The notification centre

Click the bell ({{icon:notifications}}) at the top right of the main tabs. The red number on it counts notifications you have not seen yet. The centre opens as a panel on the right; the rest of the app stays usable. It keeps your last 200 notifications, also after a restart.

  1. Mark all read clears the dots and the count on the bell. {{icon:help_outline}} opens this page, {{icon:delete_sweep}} Clear all empties the list, and {{icon:close}} closes the panel.
  2. Filter by level. Each chip shows how many notifications it has. Click one to show only that level; All shows everything. Type in Search notifications to find words in the title, message, fix or feature name.
  3. Today, Yesterday and Earlier group the list, newest first. A dot on the left marks notifications that arrived since you last looked. Hover the time to see the exact date and time.
  4. Hover an entry for its buttons: the same Open … buttons as the pop-up, Copy (copies the text, or the details when there are some) and Dismiss (removes it from the list). Clicking an entry that has an Open … button does the same as that button.
  5. Notification settings closes the panel and opens SettingsApp Settings.

While the centre is open, pop-ups fade away and new ones do not appear, so they never cover the list. Anything that arrives meanwhile shows up in the centre straight away.

An entry tagged Muted is one you silenced (by level or by feature), so it did not pop up. It is listed only while Keep muted ones in the notification centre is on.

Choose what pops up

Open SettingsApp Settings. The Notifications panel runs across the bottom of the tab. Like the rest of App Settings, changes apply when you click Save.

  1. Send a test shows one error, one warning, one info and one done pop-up using your saved settings. If you have unsaved changes, it asks you to save first.
  2. By level: one card per level. See below.
  3. By feature: silence a level for one part of Mycelian. See Silence one feature below.

By level

  • The switch at the top of each card turns pop-ups for that level on or off. Turned-off levels count as muted.
  • Stays up is how many seconds the pop-up stays on screen. Type any number of seconds (1 or more) and press Enter or click away.
  • Never dismiss keeps that level on screen until you close it. The seconds box then reads Never. Errors start with this on.

Silence one feature

Say test alerts and saved-alert pop-ups are too chatty, but you still want to hear about alert errors:

  1. Open SettingsApp Settings and scroll to By feature.
  2. Find the Alerts row and untick Info and Done. Leave Errors and Warnings ticked.
  3. Click Save.
  • Click a column header (Errors, Warnings, Info or Done) to untick that level for every feature at once. Click it again to tick it everywhere.
  • A feature with all four boxes unticked is tagged Muted.
  • The features are: App, Updates, Database, Theme, Alerts, Activity Feed, Source Settings, Source Controls, Spore Studio, Chatbot, Connectors, Statistics, Overlay server, OBS, Twitch, YouTube, Discord, Spotify, PlayStation, Streamlabs, StreamElements, Game Hooks, Files and Help. The feature name on a pop-up tells you which row to change.

The colors for each level are swatches in the Notifications group under Colors on the same tab (Success, Warning, Error, Info). These settings are saved with the theme when you click Apply, so each theme can have its own pop-up look. The built-in Dark and Light themes cannot be changed: use Save as to keep your version as a new theme. The OBS docks use the same look.

Pop-ups in OBS docks

The Activity Feed and Source Controls docks in OBS show their own small pop-ups along the bottom of the dock, for things you do in the dock (pausing alerts, skipping one, sending a control). They follow the Pop-up notifications switch and the By level settings in App Settings, and update when you save there.

  • Only errors from a dock are also sent to the app's notification centre, under Activity Feed or Source Controls. Everything else stays in the dock.
  • App pop-ups do not appear inside the docks.
  • When the dock cannot reach Mycelian, its error pop-up says so and offers Try again where that makes sense.

See Source Controls and Setting Up Browser Sources in OBS for adding the docks.

Connection notices

The status footer at the bottom of the window shows every connection change as it happens. Pop-ups are kept for changes that matter:

  • A service pops up only once it settles into a problem, such as Twitch disconnected or Spotify needs you to sign in again. Brief drops that fix themselves stay quiet. Right after Mycelian starts, services get extra time to connect before anything is reported.
  • When it recovers, you get one … reconnected pop-up, but only if you were told about the problem.
  • If the internet drops, you get one You're offline warning instead of one per service, then Back online when it returns. Services then get some time to reconnect quietly.
  • Routine states such as Connecting and Reconnecting never pop up. Watch the footer for those.

I'm not seeing notifications

No pop-ups appear at all

Check Pop-up notifications in SettingsApp Settings is on, and that you clicked Save. Then click Send a test: four pop-ups should appear in the corner chosen in Where pop-ups appear. Also check the notification centre is closed; pop-ups are hidden while it is open.

One kind of notification never pops up

Open the bell and find the entry. If it is tagged Muted, either its level is switched off under By level, or its box is unticked for that feature under By feature. The feature name on the entry tells you which row to check. If the entry is not in the centre at all and Keep muted ones in the notification centre is off, it may still be muted: turn that switch on to find out.

A pop-up disappears too fast, or never goes away

Change Stays up for that level, or switch Never dismiss on or off. Hovering a pop-up pauses its timer. Pop-ups can also close early when more arrive than Most on screen at once allows; raise it if you miss things during busy moments. Everything stays in the notification centre either way.

The same problem keeps happening but I only see one pop-up

That is on purpose. Repeats update the pop-up you already have and add ×2, ×3… See ×N: when the same thing happens again above.

A service went down but nothing popped up

Mycelian waits until the problem holds for a few seconds, and stays quiet about routine reconnecting. While the internet is down you get only You're offline. Check the status footer for the live state, and see Connection and Integration Issues.

No system notifications while Mycelian is in the tray

Turn on Minimize to tray and Desktop notifications when minimized in App Settings, then save. Only errors and warnings are sent, not muted ones, and not repeats. Your operating system must also allow notifications from Mycelian.

The OBS dock shows nothing, or an app error is missing from the dock

Docks only show pop-ups for actions taken in the dock, and app pop-ups never appear there. Dock pop-ups use the saved Pop-up notifications and By level settings. If you just changed them, save in App Settings; if the dock still ignores them, right-click the dock in OBS and choose Refresh.
